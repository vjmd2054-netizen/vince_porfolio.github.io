Case study

Customs Data Analysis

An analysis workflow designed to explore shipment and customs records, surface operational patterns, and support better reporting decisions.

PythonPandasExcelData Cleaning
View GitHub profile ↗

The objective

Organize customs and shipment data into a reliable analysis-ready dataset, then identify trends in transaction volume, processing, and recurring issues.

Approach

  • Standardize formats and validate key fields.
  • Remove duplicates and handle incomplete records.
  • Summarize volume and patterns with Excel and Pandas.

What this demonstrates

This project shows a practical end-to-end approach: cleaning operational data before analysis, then turning it into understandable summaries for business users.
